# Kevin's Coaching Page | August 2026

Hi Lane! Congrats on the opening of the Briarwood Studio space, it looks like the vision came to life! I'm sure it's a relief but also satisfying to have it installed.

Hope you've had a good summer otherwise. It's been a busy summer here, and I've been drawing a lot so I think I'll have plenty to share. There have been a couple big themes in my drawing practice recently:

- I've been encouraging myself to take a more playful approach with design while drawing. For example, bringing in more imaginative visual elements, diverging from reference more often, and making more choices based on abstract design rather than accuracy to observation. I've worked this way in the past but often felt a level of hesitation or uncertainty compared to the relative creative safety of sticking closely to the reference. I find myself thinking more about shape design, playful mark making, and pushing different types of contrast between layers of line, value, color, etc. Basically I feel like I'm experimenting with all the elements of drawing rather than only trying to understand them better or apply them accurately. Maybe what I'm describing sounds familiar to you, it's a bit difficult to put into words.

- A few artists who attend the life drawing sessions I host expressed interest in more in depth teaching, so I’ve started providing a coaching program for them similar to this one. I wouldn’t have imagined this as an option without experiencing firsthand how effective and transformative personal mentorship and direct critique can be. I’ve worked with five students so far, and I can see why you enjoy it. If someone wants to talk with me about drawing for an hour or two, I’ll jump at the chance! Of course the idea that teaching is the best way to learn is true, and I’ve been able to reinforce many of the themes we’ve talked about in coaching by sharing ideas with others. Since most of the students are in my local area, I also offer them a free life drawing session once a month, so it’s kind of a membership for the life drawing group too. It’s been a great experience and directly inspired by your coaching model!

My current goal is to continue developing my eye for design and developing confidence in my own stylistic taste and direction. I don’t know exactly how to do that but I’m enjoying the experimentation lately!
